## 1. Purpose

This standard gives customers enough published information to choose a service and price band without a lengthy back-and-forth. It also gives the Fresh Solutions team one consistent basis for fixed packages, starting prices, optional extras, and written quotations.

## 2. Pricing principles

- **Every price is in Ghana cedis.** Published prices and final invoices use GHS (GH₵). Applicable taxes are itemised separately on the invoice.
- **A fixed package needs a fixed scope.** A fixed price applies when your requirements match the package limits. “From” prices are minimums for the stated starting scope.
- **A written quote comes before work.** We confirm the scope, deliverables, timeline, exclusions, and total in writing. No project or repair work begins without your approval.
- **Changes are agreed first.** Two revision rounds are included in fixed packages. Additional or changed work is quoted before it is carried out.
- **Published prices hold for 30 days.** Prices are reviewed quarterly. An accepted written quotation stays fixed for 30 days unless the scope changes.
- **Third-party costs stay transparent.** Domains, hosting, software licences, AI/API usage, app-store fees, hardware parts, and specialist travel are disclosed and approved separately.

## 3. Default commercial terms

- Projects below GH₵20,000: 50% deposit to book the work and 50% before final handover.
- Projects of GH₵20,000 or more: 40% deposit, 30% at the agreed midpoint, and 30% before handover.
- Monthly retainers and hosting plans are billed in advance. Recurring third-party costs are shown separately.
- Repairs: the diagnosis fee is paid before assessment; the approved repair balance is due before collection or delivery.
- Work outside a package is quoted from GH₵350 per hour, except specialist security or embedded-engineering work, which is quoted in writing.
- Payment instructions are provided on the invoice. This website does not take online card payments.

## 4. Customer workflow

1. **Choose a package.** Compare published GHS prices, package limits, and optional extras without waiting for a sales call.
2. **Send the short enquiry.** Your chosen package and indicative total are carried into the project form automatically.
3. **Confirm the written quote.** We verify scope and send one clear quotation, including taxes, exclusions, and payment milestones.
4. **Approve and begin.** After your approval and first payment, we schedule the work and share progress against the agreed scope.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

### Are the published prices final?

A fixed package price is final when the requirements fit the package scope. A “from” price is the minimum for the stated starting scope. Every customer receives a written quotation before work starts, so there is one agreed final price.

### Can I choose more than one package?

Yes. The estimate builder lets you combine packages — for example, a Business Website with Website Care or an IT Support Plan with Device Setup. Recurring prices are counted for one billing period in the indicative total.

### Is the first conversation free?

A 15-minute fit call is free. Detailed advice is charged at the published advisory rate or included in a discovery package.

### Are VAT and third-party costs included?

No. Published prices exclude VAT and other statutory charges, which are itemised on the invoice. Third-party costs are confirmed with you before purchase and shown separately.

### What if I change my mind about the scope?

Tell us before the extra work is done. We issue a short written change request with the price and timeline impact, and you decide whether to proceed.

### Do I pay on the website?

No. This site publishes prices and helps you choose a package. After you approve the written quotation, payment instructions are provided on your invoice.

### What happens after I submit a package enquiry?

We check that the selected package fits your requirements, confirm any exclusions, and send a written quotation. Once you approve it and the first payment is received, work is scheduled.

## 8. Changing a published price

1. Open `lib/pricing.ts` — it is the single source of truth for the public rate card.
2. Change the relevant `price`, `unit`, `kind`, inclusions, or add-on in `SERVICE_PRICING`.
3. If commercial terms change, update `PRICING_STANDARD`, `PAYMENT_TERMS`, `PRICING_RULES`, or `PRICING_FAQS` in the same file.
4. Run `npm run pricing:export` to regenerate this document.
5. Run `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, and `npm run build` before deploying.
6. Update the `effectiveDate` when a new rate card is approved.
